This site is becoming more than a blog. The writing covers agentic systems,
inference, evaluation, and the product decisions around them, and the people
who read it tend to be building the same things. The community is the place to
compare notes.

## Where it lives

Conversations run on **GitHub Discussions**, attached to this site's
repository. Every post and video has a discussion thread at the bottom of the
page, and the discussions home has a few standing categories:

- **Ask.** Questions about anything on the site, or about problems you are
  working through. I answer; so do other readers.
- **Show and tell.** What you built, measured, or broke. Prototypes, benchmarks,
  war stories.
- **Ideas.** Tech, science, product. Half-formed thinking is welcome; that is
  what the category is for.
- **Corrections.** Something wrong in a post? Say so here or use the "Suggest an
  edit" link on the post itself.

You need a GitHub account to post. Reading is open to everyone.

[Open the discussions on GitHub →](https://github.com/linjiah/linjiah.github.io/discussions)

## House rules

- Be generous. Assume the other person is smart and busy.
- Disagree with the argument, not the person. Bring evidence or a mechanism.
- Stay on topic for the thread you are in; start a new one when the subject
  changes.
- No spam, undisclosed promotion, or recruiting pitches.
- Anything that would not be acceptable in a good engineering team meeting is
  not acceptable here. I moderate, and I remove what crosses the line.

## What comes next

As the group grows, the plan is a monthly digest of the best threads in the
[newsletter](/subscribe/), a page for projects that came out of discussions,
and live sessions around videos. If you want a hand in shaping that, say so in
Ideas.
