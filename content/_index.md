# Linjia Hu

Hi, I'm Linjia. I build AI systems that have to keep working after the demo — agentic reasoning, ML platforms, and inference stacks under real traffic.

Currently **Director of AI Systems Architecture** at **Intelligent Machines**, working on full-stack software for agentic and generative AI on proprietary silicon: distributed runtimes, optimized inference (**vLLM**, **SGLang**, **llama.cpp**, **IREE**, **OpenXLA**), and hardware-aware performance. Before this I was **Principal Applied Scientist** at **Microsoft**, technical owner of agent reasoning, orchestration, and post-training for **Microsoft Copilot** and **Azure Global Datacenter AI** — and earlier built ML platforms at **Amazon** and edge AI at **Qualcomm**.

I write here about architecture that survives contact with production: agent control flow, inference SLOs, evaluation, and the tradeoffs that only surface at scale.
