Everything I publish lands here first: long-form writing on agentic systems,
inference, and evaluation; videos once the channel is live; and short notes in
between. The email list is how you hear about it without checking back.

**What to expect.** About one email a month. New posts and videos with a
sentence on why they matter, occasionally a short note that did not need a full
post. No sponsored content.

**Privacy.** The list runs on Buttondown. Your address is used for nothing else,
there are no tracking pixels, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe.
